Pourwise privacy policy

Last updated 25 November 2026

The short version: Pourwise keeps everything on your phone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking. Nothing you enter is sent to me or to anyone else.

What Pourwise stores, and where

Pourwise stores these only on your phone, in the app's own storage:

  • Your details: height, weight, sex and where you live. They're used to estimate your blood alcohol and to show standard drinks for your country.
  • What you log: drinks, food and water and when you had them, how you felt, how your mornings went, and any plans, targets and prices you choose to add.
  • Your settings.

I never see any of it.

What Pourwise doesn't do

  • It doesn't send your information anywhere. The app makes no internet requests.
  • It has no accounts or sign-in, no ads, no analytics, no crash reporting and no third-party tracking code.
  • It doesn't sell or share your information. I never receive it, so there's nothing to share.
  • It doesn't use your location. You choose where you live. Pourwise suggests a country from your phone's time zone and language, worked out on the phone.

Backups

  • Download a backup saves a file on your phone (in Documents/Pourwise on Android). It stays where you put it, and only goes anywhere else if you send it somewhere yourself.
  • Pourwise keeps a weekly copy of your data inside the app. On Android it also saves that copy in Documents/Pourwise.
  • Android's own backup: if your phone backs up apps to your Google account, Android may include Pourwise's data. That backup is between you and Google, under Google's privacy policy, and you can turn it off in your phone's settings.

Permissions on Android

  • Notifications: only for the morning reminder, and only if you turn it on.
  • Start when the phone restarts: so a morning reminder you've set still arrives after a restart.
  • Internet: included by the framework Pourwise is built with. Pourwise doesn't use it.

The Call button in the danger panel opens your phone's dialler with the emergency number for your country. Nothing is called until you press call.

The web version

Pourwise also runs in a web browser. It works the same way: your information stays in that browser on your device. The site is hosted by GitHub Pages, and like any website host, GitHub may record technical details such as your IP address when the page loads. See GitHub's privacy statement. Pourwise adds no tracking of its own.

Deleting your information

  • Uninstalling Pourwise deletes everything it stores in the app. Backup files you saved stay until you delete them.
  • In the web version, clearing the site's data in your browser does the same.
  • You can delete any past night in Your week.

Who it's for

Pourwise is for adults of legal drinking age where they live. It isn't meant for anyone younger.

An estimate, not advice

Pourwise estimates blood alcohol from what you log. It isn't a breathalyser or medical advice, and it must never be used to decide whether you're OK to drive.

Changes
